Your first match in three steps

Your goal: reduce the enemy base to 0 HP.

  1. Draw & deploy

    Drawing and playing cards both cost Action Points (AP). Select a card, then a highlighted square to deploy it. For spells, follow the targeting prompt.

  2. Start battle

    When you are ready, choose Start battle. Select a friendly unit: green squares are legal moves, red squares are attack targets.

  3. End turn

    Most units can move OR attack once per turn. Assassins can attack after moving. End your turn when you are done, then let the AI act.

Cost: your first two paid draws each cost 1 AP; later draws cost 2 AP. You can hold up to 7 cards.

Cards & units: ATK is attack, HP is health, RNG is range, and AP is action points. Select a card to read its full abilities.

Cancel: choose Cancel or press Esc. A disabled button means its requirements are not met.
