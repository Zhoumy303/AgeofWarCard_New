AGE OF WAR — BILINGUAL EDITION

Open game-en.html to play the bilingual edition.
Use 中文 / English in the upper-right corner to switch languages.
Your preference is remembered; switching keeps the current game and progress.
Text printed inside card artwork remains English.
Open game.html to play the preserved Chinese edition.
Keep both HTML files in this project folder, alongside css/, js/ and lib/.

The English edition includes translated menus, battle guidance, card rules,
bosses, adventure events, help, save management and developer tools.
The nine supplied PNG card faces are stored unchanged in lib/img/cards-en/.
Select one of these cards, then choose "View full card" to inspect the artwork.
Card artwork is shown in full; current AP costs and unit stats are separate.

Gameplay rules and balance match the Chinese edition. Printed artwork has some
differences from the implemented rules, notably Archer, Shadow Lord and Frost
Dragon. The English details and the "LIVE GAME RULES" panel describe the actual
game behavior. Artwork pixels have not been edited.

English progress and AI statistics use a separate aow-en: storage namespace.
Starting, continuing or clearing English saves does not alter Chinese saves.

Verification: 80 automated tests passed, including Chinese regression tests,
English startup and text checks, all three starter decks, asset integrity,
save isolation and preservation of the original Chinese source files.
Four seeded AI comparisons matched the Chinese game at every turn boundary
through 16 player-turn switches each. This is a regression check, not a new
balance study or exhaustive coverage of every possible match.

Run the checks with Node.js:
node --test tests/game.test.cjs tests/combat-regression.test.cjs tests/multiplayer.test.cjs tests/player-experience.test.cjs tests/english.test.cjs tests/english-parity.test.cjs

Browser checks: quick match, full card viewer, deploy, move, AI turn and return
of control, hero selection, adventure events and deck inspection; desktop and
390-pixel mobile layouts. Card art loaded at its original 1000 x 1546 pixels.
